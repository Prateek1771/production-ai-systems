Satya Nadella is an Indian-American business executive who became the CEO of Microsoft in 2014, succeeding Steve Ballmer. Before becoming CEO, Nadella led several engineering and cloud computing initiatives within Microsoft, helping shape the company's enterprise strategy.

After taking leadership, Nadella transformed Microsoft's corporate culture by encouraging collaboration, innovation, and a greater focus on cloud computing. Under his leadership, Microsoft Azure grew into one of the world's leading cloud platforms and became a central part of Microsoft's business.

Nadella played a major role in negotiating Microsoft's multi-billion-dollar investment in OpenAI. He recognized that advances in generative AI would become strategically important and pushed Microsoft to build a long-term partnership with the organization. He also maintains a close professional relationship with OpenAI CEO Sam Altman.

Today, Satya Nadella is widely regarded as one of the most influential technology leaders. His decisions have strengthened Microsoft's position in cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and enterprise software while expanding partnerships with companies such as OpenAI and NVIDIA.
