## ENTERPRISE RISK AI BUILD & EXPLAIN

Aug 20, 2026 08:30 PM

### About this webinar

AI models are increasingly used to assess financial, operational, and business risks, but accuracy alone isn't enough. Enterprises need transparent, explainable, and trustworthy AI that stakeholders can understand and confidently use for decision-making. White-Box AI and explainability techniques help bridge the gap between model performance and business trust.

Join Krish Naik Academy for a hands-on technical session on Building and Explaining an Enterprise Risk AI. Learn how to build a risk prediction model, interpret its decisions using SHAP, validate performance, and understand what it takes to move AI prototypes from local notebooks to cloud-based production environments.

### Key Learning Objectives

1. Enterprise Risk Modeling: Understand how AI predicts financial and operational risks using behavioral data.
2. White-Box AI: Learn the principles of interpretable and explainable machine learning.
3. Risk Model Development: Build a baseline prediction model using Scikit-learn.
4. SHAP Visualizations: Interpret predictions through global and local feature importance.
5. Model Validation: Evaluate performance and build trustworthy AI systems.
6. Cloud Deployment: Understand the transition from local AI prototypes to production environments.

### WHO SHOULD ATTEND?

Data Scientists, ML Engineers, AI Engineers, Risk Analysts, Financial Analysts, Python Developers, and professionals interested in Explainable AI (XAI).

### WHAT YOU'LL GAIN

Gain practical experience in building enterprise risk models, applying White-Box AI principles, interpreting predictions with SHAP, validating models, and understanding production deployment strategies.

### KEY TAKEAWAYS

Enterprise Risk AI, White-Box AI, Scikit-learn, SHAP, Explainable AI, Model Validation, and Cloud Deployment Fundamentals.
